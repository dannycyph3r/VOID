01Color

  • Mint (flare) marks what matters now: selection, the primary action, trajectory, beacon, time, score. Never body text, borders at rest or headings.
  • Alarm is the only other hue: event horizons, low fuel, failure. Always paired with a shape (teeth, ✕) so it reads without color.
  • Ratios are against Void. Mint and Alarm differ in lightness (12.9 vs 5.2), not just hue.
