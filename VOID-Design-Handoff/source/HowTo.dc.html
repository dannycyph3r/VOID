How to Play

01Aim and launch

Set angle and power from the pad. The dashed line previews the first seconds of flight. Launching is free; the clock starts when the probe leaves the pad.

02Gravity

Every planet pulls the probe toward it, and the pull grows sharply as you get closer. Dotted rings mark equal pull; tighter rings mean stronger pull. Passing close to a planet bends and speeds up your path: a gravity assist.

03Thrusters and fuel

In flight, each press fires one burst in that direction: 0.28 s, 2.4 u of fuel. Hold to repeat. Each stage starts with its own tank. At 0 u the probe can only coast. Fuel below 20 u turns the gauge red.

04Gates and beacon

Gates open and close on a fixed cycle; the bar above a gate shows how long it stays open. Pass through every gate, then touch the mint beacon to complete the stage.

05Singularities

A singularity pulls far harder than any planet. Its toothed red ring is the event horizon: cross it and the run fails. The dashed outer ring marks where its pull begins.

06Debt Score

Score = Seconds × 10 + Fuel × 25

Lower is better. Every second and every unit of fuel adds to what the run owes. Failed runs don't score. Each stage has a par to beat.

e.g. 38.42 s × 10 + 21.6 u × 25 = 924

08Daily and ranked runs

  • One daily stage, identical for everyone, from 00:00 to 24:00 UTC.
  • Fly it as often as you like; your best run of the day is ranked.
  • Only completed runs are ranked. Each is verified by replaying your inputs.
  • Runs are saved on your device and submit when you're online.
